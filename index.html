How to play

Tap an arrow to move it. Remove all arrows to clear the cube.

An arrow moves until it leaves the cube or hits another arrow.

  • Two-ended arrow

    Tap the green half to move the green end first. Tap the yellow half to move the yellow end first.

  • Blue turn marker

    The front end turns in the marker direction. The marker does not change.

  • Purple turn marker

    The front end turns in the marker direction. The marker then reverses direction. If the arrow leaves the cube, the marker keeps its new direction. After a crash, the marker returns to its previous direction.

  • Orange slide edge

    The arrow moves around this edge to the next face. The edge works in both directions.

  • Crash

    The arrow turns red and moves back. Each crash removes one heart. With no hearts, use a reset or restart the same puzzle.

  • Reset

    A reset restores five hearts and keeps your progress. You can have four resets. One reset returns every three hours.

  • Pale arrow

    This arrow is on the far side. Turn the cube to select it.

Controls and hints

Drag to turn the cube. Twist two fingers to turn the cube in the screen plane. Pinch or scroll to change the view size.

Select Hint to find a clear path. Hints do not use hearts or resets.

Keyboard: Select the cube. Use the arrow keys to turn it. Press H for a hint. Press Enter to move the selected arrow.
