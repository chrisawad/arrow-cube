 SIX SIDES. ONE SIMPLE RULE.

Follow the arrow.

Each line can only move in the direction of its arrowhead. If another line is in its way, clear that line first. A clear arrow slides off the edge.

Drag the cube to find the paths on every face. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Every puzzle has a solution, and hints are always free.

Keyboard: focus the cube, then use arrow keys to rotate. Press H for a hint and Enter to release the highlighted arrow.

Your exact puzzle and progress are saved automatically in this browser. Once installed, the game works offline.
